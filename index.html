Alpha-Gal Syndrome
Context

Definition

  • IgE allergy to galactose-α-1,3-galactose in nonprimate mammalian tissue/products.4
  • Needs compatible history + alpha-gal IgE; sensitization alone ≠ AGS.5

Context

  • Tick bites induce alpha-gal IgE.5
  • Oral: delayed 3–6 h. Parenteral: may be immediate.3

Relevance to facial plastics

  • Gelatin, collagen, thrombin, grafts, implants, sutures, injectables.9
  • Animal origin often unlabeled; may vary by lot.8

Epidemiology

  • 90,018 suspected cases, 2017–2022.6
  • Up to 450,000 US affected since 2010 (CDC estimate).6
  • Serosurvey highs: AR 31.2%, MO 26.0% (blood donors, 2024–2025).13
  • Seroprevalence = sensitization, not disease.13

Clinical Decision
Action

Takeaway: SCREEN · SUBSTITUTE · PREVENT

Preprocedure checklist

1
Confirm delayed meat/gelatin reactions, tick exposure, prior perioperative reactions.
2
Review severity, cofactors, alpha-gal IgE.
3
Audit drugs, hemostats, grafts, implants, sutures.
4
Verify animal origin (pharmacy/manufacturer).
5
Prefer nonmammalian/synthetic.
6
Document readiness + shared decision.

Clinical caveats

  • No universal IgE threshold.
  • Tolerance varies by route/product.
  • Prior tolerance ≠ future safety.
  • Verify every formulation.

Route matters

Oral

Delayed 3–6 h

Parenteral

May be immediate

Implant

Persistent exposure — retained material may act as a nidus7,12

Persistence = nidus risk

  • Risk tracks how long mammalian material stays in tissue.7,12
  • Retained (high): xenogeneic ADM, xenograft bone/cartilage/tendon, bioprosthetic patches/valves — months–years.7,12
  • Resorbable (injection only): collagen filler carriers (e.g. Bellafill carrier resorbs ~1 mo → PMMA only). Late nodule = PMMA, not alpha-gal.11

Material Risk by Evidence
Action
Material / exposureEvidenceResponse
Gelatin hemostats; animal thrombin Demonstrated Human reactions reported. Use recombinant/nonmammalian.7,9
Bovine/porcine matrices, xenografts Demonstrated (analogous implants) Alpha-gal persists post-decellularization; facial ADM untested. Confirm species.7,8
Porcine UFH; high-dose IV UFH Clinical signal Varies by titer; highest with IV. Check sIgE; risk-stratify, don't universally exclude.
Bovine-collagen PMMA filler; facial ADM; catgut; collagen implants/adhesives Mammalian / untested Composition established, brand assays absent. FDA: PMMA carrier = bovine collagen. Verify IFU.10
Autologous / human; confirmed synthetic / nonmammalian Lower risk Autologous fat/cartilage/fascia; select HA, CaHA, PLLA; synthetic sutures; cyanoacrylate. Verify every formulation.

Evidence gap

No study quantifies alpha-gal reactions in cosmetic/aesthetic procedures. Alpha-gal not directly assayed in any facial plastics brand; brand risk inferred from sourcing. Extrapolated from anesthesia, cardiac surgery, in-vitro, and animal data. “Lower risk” = sourcing, not a verified assay.

Specialist input required when mammalian material is unavoidable; premedication does not reliably prevent anaphylaxis.
